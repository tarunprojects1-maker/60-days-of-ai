Day 48 – The Verdict Engine
Objective

The objective of today's experiment was to understand how Artificial Intelligence can assist in evaluating multiple options using structured decision-making, weighted criteria, and evidence-based analysis. The application focused on comparing alternatives, ranking them according to user-defined priorities, and generating transparent recommendations supported by research.

What I Did

In today's experiment, I generated The Verdict Engine application as an HTML project using Claude AI. After opening the application in my browser, I selected multiple options to compare and completed the guided comparison workflow.

I adjusted the importance of different evaluation criteria using weight controls and observed how the rankings changed in real time. I also reviewed the supporting sources for each data point and explored the "How this was researched" panel to understand the research methodology, evidence collection, and conflict resolution process used by the application.

Finally, I reviewed the final recommendation dashboard, captured screenshots of the comparison workflow, and documented the complete learning experience in my GitHub repository.

Key Learning Outcomes
Understood how AI supports evidence-based decision-making.
Learned how weighted criteria influence comparison results.
Explored transparent AI reasoning and source verification.
Improved my understanding of ranking algorithms and evaluation methods.
Learned the importance of explainable AI in decision-support systems.
Strengthened my knowledge of interactive AI-powered comparison applications.
