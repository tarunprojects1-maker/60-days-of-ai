# Day 30 – Supply Chain Optimizer

## Objective

The objective of today's experiment was to understand how different supply chain decisions influence overall business performance. The simulator demonstrated how supplier selection, factory location, warehouse planning, transportation methods, and inventory strategies work together to build an efficient and resilient supply chain.

---

## What I Did

In this experiment, I generated the Supply Chain Optimizer as an HTML application and tested it in my browser. I started by reviewing the beginner-friendly introduction and analyzing the randomly generated company profile. Based on the business scenario, I selected a supplier strategy, factory location, warehouse strategy, transportation method, and inventory management approach.

As I made each decision, I observed how the business metrics changed in real time. I reviewed the final optimization dashboard, analyzed the overall supply chain score, identified the strengths and weaknesses of my strategy, and studied the recommended improvements. To compare different outcomes, I restarted the simulator and tested another company profile using a different combination of strategies. Finally, I captured screenshots of the important stages and documented the complete learning experience.
