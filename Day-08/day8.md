# Day 8 – Personal Environmental Health Analyzer

## Introduction

Today, I explored how AI can be used to transform environmental data into an interactive analytical dashboard.

The objective of this activity was to understand how Large Language Models can assist in data analysis, visualization, dashboard design, and report generation by creating a complete Environmental Health Analyzer application.

---

## Activity Performed

As part of today's challenge, I used Claude to generate a complete Environmental Health Analyzer dashboard.

The application was designed to analyze environmental conditions using air quality and water quality information and present the results through an interactive dashboard.

The generated application included:

* Environmental Health Metrics
* AQI Analysis
* City Comparisons
* Health Risk Indicators
* Environmental Health Scores
* Interactive Charts
* Personalized Recommendations
* City Ranking Analysis

The final result was generated as a downloadable HTML application that could be opened and interacted with directly in a web browser.

---

## What I Learned

Through this activity, I learned how AI can assist in:

* Data analysis and interpretation
* Dashboard generation
* Frontend application creation
* Interactive visualization design
* Environmental health reporting

I also observed how a well-structured prompt can guide AI to generate a complete analytical solution rather than a simple response.

## Key Observations

One of the most interesting observations was how multiple data points could be combined into a single dashboard to provide meaningful environmental insights.

The dashboard transformed raw information into:
