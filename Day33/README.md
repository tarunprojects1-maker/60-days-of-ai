# Day 33 – Media Integrity Analyzer

## Objective

The objective of today's experiment was to understand how AI can help identify misleading information, emotional manipulation, and bias in digital media. The simulator focused on evaluating news headlines, detecting emotional influence, and measuring media integrity through interactive analysis and real-time scoring.

---

## What I Did

In this experiment, I generated the Media Integrity Analyzer as an HTML application and tested it in my browser. I began by selecting a preferred color theme and exploring the simulator interface. I then completed the Headline Detective challenge by analyzing a news headline, reviewing its credibility, and comparing it with a rewritten, more balanced version.

Next, I completed the Emotion Detector challenge, where I identified emotional manipulation techniques used in different media content. As I progressed through the simulation, I observed how the Media Integrity metrics updated in real time based on my responses. After completing all the activities, I reviewed the final Media Integrity Dashboard, analyzed the overall integrity score and recommendations, and replayed the simulator with a different randomized scenario to compare the results.

Finally, I captured screenshots of the important stages and documented the complete learning experience.

---

## Key Learning Outcomes

* Understood how AI can assist in detecting misleading or biased media content.
* Learned how headlines can influence readers through emotional manipulation.
* Explored different techniques used in sensationalized and clickbait content.
* Observed how credibility, bias, and emotional influence affect media integrity scores.
* Learned the importance of critical thinking and fact-based analysis when consuming digital media.
* Improved my understanding of AI-powered media analysis and interactive learning applications.
