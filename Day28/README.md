# Day 28 – Hospital Admission Readiness Simulator

## Objective

The objective of today's experiment was to understand how hospitals evaluate whether a patient is ready for admission before treatment begins. The simulator demonstrated how factors such as clinical documentation, insurance verification, prior authorization, bed availability, and governance checks influence the final admission decision.

---

## What I Did

In this experiment, I generated the Hospital Admission Readiness Simulator as an HTML application and tested it in my browser. I configured a patient admission scenario by selecting the healthcare provider, attending physician, diagnosis, admission type, insurance payer, prior authorization status, and admission date.

After completing the setup, I analyzed the initial admission readiness score and identified the risks affecting the admission process. I then completed the required workflow actions, including resolving prior authorization, improving documentation, verifying insurance details, and reducing clinical and operational risks. I also reviewed the Governance Snapshot and observed how the readiness score improved before reaching the final admission decision.

To better understand different outcomes, I restarted the simulator and tested another admission scenario. Finally, I captured screenshots of the important stages and documented the overall learning from the simulation.

---

## Key Learning Outcomes

* Understood how hospitals assess admission readiness before patient admission.
* Learned the importance of prior authorization in the healthcare admission process.
* Explored how documentation, insurance verification, and clinical reviews impact admission decisions.
* Observed how workflow actions improve the overall readiness score.
* Understood the role of governance checks in ensuring safe and efficient patient admissions.
* Improved my understanding of workflow-based healthcare applications and interactive simulations.
