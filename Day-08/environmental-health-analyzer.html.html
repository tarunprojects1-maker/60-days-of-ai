Daily Actions for Cuttack Residents
Check AQI every morning before going out — aim to go out only when AQI < 100
Wear N95/KN95 masks outdoors, especially 7–10 AM when PM levels peak
Avoid main roads and high-traffic areas; use back routes when possible
Keep windows closed during morning and evening rush hours
Shower and change clothes after extended outdoor exposure
Indoor Air Improvements
Use a HEPA air purifier, especially in bedroom — run it at least 8 hours/day
Add indoor plants: Spider Plant, Peace Lily, Snake Plant help filter PM2.5
Avoid incense, candles, and mosquito coils — they spike indoor PM2.5 by 200–400%
Ventilate kitchen well; use exhaust fans while cooking
Vacuum with HEPA filter; damp-mop floors to trap settled particulates
Outdoor Activity Guidance
Exercise outdoors only when AQI < 100; prefer 6–7 PM (lower peaks in Cuttack)
Shift high-intensity workouts indoors during AQI > 100 days
Children and elderly should avoid all outdoor exercise above AQI 100
Parks near water bodies (Mahanadi ghats) tend to have marginally better air
