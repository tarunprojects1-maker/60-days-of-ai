# Day 29 – Operation Lifeline: Supply Chain Crisis Simulator

## Objective

The objective of today's experiment was to understand how businesses respond to unexpected supply chain disruptions and how strategic decision-making helps organizations maintain operations during challenging situations. The simulator focused on crisis management, leadership, supplier negotiations, and AI-driven business improvements.

---

## What I Did

In this experiment, I generated the Operation Lifeline Simulator as an HTML application and tested it in my browser. The simulation presented a randomly generated company profile along with a supply chain crisis that required immediate action.

I analyzed the assigned business scenario, selected appropriate crisis response strategies, completed supplier negotiation rounds, and answered leadership questions from the CEO's perspective. I also evaluated different AI-based supply chain investments to improve operational efficiency and business resilience. After completing all stages, I reviewed the executive dashboard, analyzed the overall business performance, and restarted the simulator to explore another scenario with different outcomes.

Finally, I captured screenshots of the important stages and documented the learning gained from the complete simulation.

---

## Key Learning Outcomes

* Understood how supply chain disruptions impact business operations.

* Explored supplier negotiation techniques in challenging situations.
* Understood the importance of leadership during operational disruptions.
