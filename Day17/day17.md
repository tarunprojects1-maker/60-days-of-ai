# Day 17 – Vehicle Cost Analysis Dashboard

## Objective

Today's experiment focused on using AI to transform raw data into meaningful visual insights through an interactive dashboard.

## What I Explored

I worked with a CSV dataset and used Claude AI to generate a complete HTML dashboard automatically. Instead of manually analyzing rows of data, I explored how AI can process structured datasets and present information through visual reports, charts, KPI cards, and analytical summaries.

This experiment helped me understand how AI can assist in converting raw information into decision-friendly dashboards with minimal effort.

## What I Learned

* Learned how AI can analyze structured datasets and identify useful metrics automatically.
* Explored AI-generated dashboard creation using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and SVG visualizations.
* Understood how different data points can be transformed into meaningful business insights.
* Learned how prompt engineering influences the quality and structure of generated analytical reports.
* Gained practical exposure to AI-assisted reporting and visualization workflows.

## Key Takeaway

This experiment showed me how AI can reduce the effort required to build dashboards and perform data analysis. Instead of spending time manually creating reports, AI can help generate structured visual outputs that can be further customized and integrated into real-world projects.

## Outcome

Successfully generated and reviewed an interactive Vehicle Cost Analysis Dashboard using AI-powered data analysis techniques, improving my understanding of dashboard automation, data visualization, and practical AI applications.
