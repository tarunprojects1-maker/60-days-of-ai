# Day 21 - Digital Privacy Dashboard

## Objective

Today's experiment focused on understanding digital privacy, online exposure, and how personal data can be visualized through an interactive dashboard.

## What I Explored

I generated and tested a Digital Privacy Dashboard using AI. The dashboard analyzed digital footprint indicators, privacy exposure levels, company data collection patterns, risk factors, and privacy improvement opportunities through multiple visual components.

## What I Learned

* Learned how digital footprints are formed through multiple online services.
* Explored privacy scoring and exposure analysis concepts.
* Understood ecosystem concentration and data collection patterns.
* Learned how dashboards can simplify complex privacy information.
* Improved understanding of privacy awareness and digital risk evaluation.
* Explored how AI can generate advanced analytics dashboards from structured datasets.

## Key Takeaway

This experiment demonstrated how AI can transform raw datasets into meaningful dashboards that help users understand privacy exposure, online risks, and opportunities to improve digital security awareness.

## Outcome

Successfully generated, tested, and documented a Digital Privacy Dashboard featuring privacy scores, exposure heatmaps, company exposure rankings, risk analysis, digital twin profiling, and privacy improvement recommendations.
