Verdict: E85 is the cleanest fuel on this list and easy on upkeep, but its real-world cost per km is the worst of the five — so it's a choice worth making for emissions, not for your wallet, unless pump price falls under ₹79.11/L.

CNG
  • ✅Cheapest combustion fuel at ₹3.33/km
  • ✅Cleaner burn — 0.125 kg CO₂/km
  • ❌Slowest refuel of any liquid/gas fuel (8 min)
  • ❌Cylinder eats into storage and range
🚗Best for high-mileage commuters near CNG pumps
Diesel
  • ✅Strong mileage at 19.6 km/L
  • ✅Quick 5-minute refuel, wide availability
  • ❌Highest emissions — 0.179 kg CO₂/km
  • ❌Highest upkeep — ₹1.01/km maintenance
🚗Best for long-haul, heavy-load riders
