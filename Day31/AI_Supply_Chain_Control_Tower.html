How to Play

You are the Head of Operations for a global supply chain network. Over a 3 minute shift, disruption alerts will appear — port congestion, supplier delays, stockouts, and more.

Your job

  • Each alert has its own countdown. Pick an action before it expires.
  • Actions are ranked internally: some are the strongest fix, some are workable but costlier, some barely help, and ignoring is always worst.
  • Unresolved alerts auto-expire as an "Ignore" outcome — the worst result.
  • Some outcomes have delayed follow-on effects a few seconds later.

KPIs to protect

  • Service Level, Customer Satisfaction, Inventory Health, Transport Efficiency — keep these high.
  • Operating Cost — every action spends money; bad handling spends more.
  • Revenue Protected — grows when you handle things well.

Difficulty

Alerts arrive faster and more can be active at once as the clock runs down. Stay ahead of the queue.
