# Day 32 – Marketing Strategy Simulator

## Objective

The objective of today's experiment was to understand how AI can assist in building an effective marketing strategy for a business or personal brand. The simulator focused on identifying the target audience, selecting the right marketing platforms, creating content pillars, and generating a structured marketing roadmap to support business growth.

---

## What I Did

In this experiment, I generated the Marketing Strategy Simulator as an HTML application and tested it in my browser. I started by selecting a marketing mode and providing the required business and brand information. Based on the selected profile, I analyzed the target audience and explored the most suitable digital marketing platforms.

Next, I selected three content pillars that aligned with the brand's goals and generated a 30-day marketing roadmap. During the simulation, I responded to a randomized marketing event and observed how different decisions affected the overall marketing strategy. Finally, I reviewed the Growth Report, analyzed the performance insights, studied the reusable Claude prompts provided throughout the simulator, and replayed the simulation using another scenario to compare the results.

I captured screenshots of the important stages and documented the complete learning experience.

## Key Learning Outcomes

* Understood how AI can support marketing strategy planning.
