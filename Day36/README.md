# Day 36 – Cognitive Pattern Explorer

## Objective

The objective of today's experiment was to understand how different thinking patterns influence decision-making under calm and stressful situations. The simulator provided an interactive experience to explore cognitive behavior, recognize thinking tendencies, and reflect on how our responses change based on different scenarios.

---

## What I Did

In this experiment, I generated the Cognitive Pattern Explorer as an HTML application and tested it in my browser. I started by selecting **Calm Mode** and completed a series of interactive activities designed to evaluate my thinking process.

In Chapter 1, I responded to everyday scenarios by selecting the options that best matched my approach. In Chapter 2, I completed drag-and-drop activities that required organizing ideas and identifying logical patterns. In Chapter 3, I arranged my thinking sequence to understand how I process information before making decisions.

After completing all three chapters, I reviewed my personalized Reflection Journal and Thinking Profile, which summarized my cognitive tendencies, strengths, and areas for improvement. To compare my responses, I replayed the simulator in **Stress Mode** and observed how different situations influenced my decision-making process. Finally, I captured screenshots of the important stages and documented the overall learning from the simulation.
