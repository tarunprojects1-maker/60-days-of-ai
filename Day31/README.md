# Day 31 – AI Supply Chain Control Tower

## Objective

The objective of today's experiment was to understand how an AI-powered Supply Chain Control Tower helps organizations monitor operations, identify disruptions, prioritize incidents, and make timely decisions to improve overall supply chain performance. The simulation demonstrated how real-time monitoring and data-driven actions contribute to efficient supply chain management.

---

## What I Did

In this experiment, I generated the AI Supply Chain Control Tower as an HTML application and tested it in my browser. After reviewing the simulator instructions, I monitored incoming operational alerts and analyzed each incident based on its urgency and business impact.

For every alert, I selected the most appropriate corrective action and observed how my decisions influenced key business performance indicators (KPIs). As multiple incidents appeared simultaneously, I prioritized them according to their severity and managed them efficiently to minimize operational disruptions. After completing the simulation, I reviewed the final operational performance dashboard, analyzed my overall performance, and replayed the simulator with a different scenario to compare the results.

Finally, I captured screenshots of the important stages and documented the complete learning experience.

---

## Key Learning Outcomes

* Understood how a Supply Chain Control Tower monitors real-time business operations.
* Learned how to prioritize operational incidents based on urgency and business impact.
* Explored different corrective actions for managing supply chain disruptions.
* Observed how business decisions directly affect operational KPIs.
* Understood the importance of real-time monitoring and decision-making in supply chain management.
* Improved my understanding of AI-powered operational control systems and business simulations.
