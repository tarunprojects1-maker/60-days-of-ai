Day 50 – Defend Your Experience
Objective

The objective of today's experiment was to understand how Artificial Intelligence can support interview preparation by analyzing a resume or portfolio and generating personalized interview questions. The application focused on helping users confidently explain their projects, technical skills, and professional experiences while providing AI-powered feedback to improve interview performance.

What I Did

In today's experiment, I generated the Defend Your Experience application as an HTML project using Claude AI. After opening the application in my browser, I uploaded my resume and explored the AI-powered interview preparation workflow.

The application analyzed my resume and generated personalized interview questions based on my projects, technical skills, and experience. I practiced answering these questions and reviewed the AI-generated Defense Report, which evaluated my communication, technical knowledge, confidence, and explanation quality. The report also highlighted strengths and provided suggestions for improving future interview responses.

Finally, I captured screenshots of the interview session, AI analysis, Defense Report, and overall performance summary, and documented the complete learning experience in my GitHub repository.

Key Learning Outcomes
Understood how AI can personalize interview preparation.
Learned to explain technical projects more confidently.
Improved communication and storytelling during technical interviews.
Explored AI-generated feedback for strengthening interview performance.
Gained practical experience with resume-based interview simulations.
Strengthened my understanding of AI-powered career development tools.
