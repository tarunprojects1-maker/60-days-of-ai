Tips, Checklist & Resources

Freelance financial literacy, distilled.

Financial tips for freelancers

Pay yourself in two transfers

Split every incoming payment immediately: one transfer to your tax envelope, one to your operating account. Doing it at the moment of deposit removes the decision later.

Invoice on a schedule, not a whim

Irregular invoicing creates irregular income. A fixed billing day each month smooths your cash flow more than almost any other habit.

Separate bank accounts, always

A dedicated business checking account makes deduction tracking and quarterly tax estimates dramatically simpler at filing time.

Price for slow months

Your rate should account for unbilled admin time, gaps between contracts, and the buffer you're not currently earning interest on.
