# Day 37 – Task Compass

## Objective

The objective of today's experiment was to understand how tasks are assigned, managed, and coordinated within an organization. The simulator focused on identifying task ownership, routing work to the appropriate teams, improving cross-functional collaboration, and developing organizational thinking through interactive workplace scenarios.

---

## What I Did

In this experiment, I generated the Task Compass application as an HTML project and tested it in my browser. I started by selecting a workplace type and explored different organizational scenarios designed to simulate real business environments.

In Stage 1, I identified the appropriate owner for each workplace task by assigning responsibilities to the correct department or team. In Stage 2, I completed the task routing activities by directing work requests through the correct organizational workflow. In Stage 3, I participated in the Collaboration Challenge, where I analyzed situations that required multiple departments to work together to achieve a common objective.

After completing all stages, I reviewed the Organizational Thinking Dashboard, which evaluated my task management, collaboration, and decision-making skills. To compare different organizational structures, I replayed the simulator using another workplace type and observed how responsibilities and workflows changed. Finally, I captured screenshots of the important stages and documented the complete learning experience.

---

## Key Learning Outcomes

* Understood how organizations assign ownership and responsibility for different tasks.
* Learned the importance of routing work to the appropriate department for efficient operations.
* Explored how cross-functional collaboration improves productivity and problem-solving.
* Improved my understanding of organizational workflows and decision-making processes.
* Learned how AI-powered simulations can simplify workplace management concepts through interactive learning.
