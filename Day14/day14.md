# Day 14 - AI Job Red Flag Detector

## Objective

Analyze a Cloud Architect Internship job description and company information using AI to identify potential hiring risks, red flags, and positive signals before applying.

## Job Role Analyzed

Cloud Architect Intern

## Company Analyzed

CloudNova Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

## Overall Risk Score

22/100 (Low Risk)

## Top Red Flags Identified

- Hybrid work schedule was not clearly defined.
- PPO is performance-based and not guaranteed.
- Preferred skills such as Docker, Kubernetes, and Terraform may create additional learning pressure.
- Stipend may be modest for Bengaluru living expenses.
- Company is relatively young and long-term stability depends on business growth.

## Positive Signals

- Salary range clearly disclosed.
- Internship requirements are realistic for students.
- Structured mentorship program available.
- No toxic workplace language detected.
- Growing company with increasing cloud projects.
- Hands-on AWS responsibilities clearly mentioned.

## Risk Breakdown

| Category | Risk Level |
|-----------|-----------|
| Requirements | Low |
| Culture | Low |
| Remote/Hybrid | Medium |
| Hiring | Low |
| Company | Low |

## Final Verdict

Apply

The internship appears to be a genuine opportunity with realistic expectations, clear responsibilities, structured mentorship, and transparent compensation.

## Key Learnings

- Learned how to identify hiring risks before applying.
- Understood how AI can detect potential red flags in job descriptions.
- Learned to evaluate company credibility and workplace signals.
- Gained insights into cloud internship requirements and hiring practices.
- Learned how to prepare smart interview questions for recruiters.

## Outcome

Successfully generated an AI-powered Job Red Flag Analysis Report and evaluated the overall suitability of a Cloud Architect Internship opportunity.
