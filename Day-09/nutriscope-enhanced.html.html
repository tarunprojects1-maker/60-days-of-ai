⚠ Educational Disclaimer
NutriScope is an educational tool and does not constitute medical or dietetic advice. Nutritional targets are estimates based on widely used formulas (Mifflin-St Jeor for BMR; standard ICMR/WHO RDI values). Individual needs vary. Always consult a registered dietitian or physician before making significant dietary changes, especially if you have a medical condition, are pregnant, or are on medication. Data entered is processed locally in your browser and is not transmitted anywhere.

• Carbs: 45–65% of TDEE; Fat: 20–35% of TDEE
