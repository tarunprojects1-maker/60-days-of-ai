# Day 34 – Marketing Detective

## Objective

The objective of today's experiment was to understand how AI can be used to analyze marketing campaigns, identify common marketing mistakes, and improve decision-making through evidence-based investigation. The simulator focused on evaluating campaign performance, organizing marketing evidence, identifying the root cause of poor results, and learning from expert recommendations.

---

## What I Did

In this experiment, I generated the Marketing Detective application as an HTML project and tested it in my browser. After selecting my preferred theme, I accepted a marketing investigation case and analyzed the campaign using the investigation board.

I carefully reviewed the available evidence, organized the evidence cards, and identified the primary marketing mistake based on the campaign data. After submitting my findings, I compared my analysis with the expert explanation and final verdict to understand the correct reasoning behind the campaign outcome.

Next, I reviewed the Marketing Learning Report, which highlighted the key insights, recommendations, and lessons from the investigation. To explore different scenarios, I replayed the simulator with another randomized marketing case and compared the results. Finally, I captured screenshots of the important stages and documented the complete learning experience.
