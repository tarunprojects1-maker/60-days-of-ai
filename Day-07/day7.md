# Day 7 – Claude Usage Strategy

## Introduction

Today, I learned how to use Claude more effectively by understanding the strengths of different Claude models and effort levels.

Before this activity, I mainly focused on obtaining answers from AI. However, this exercise helped me understand that selecting the right model and reasoning effort can significantly improve productivity, output quality, and resource usage.

---

## Activity Performed

As part of today's challenge, I installed and explored the Claude Counter extension to better understand Claude usage limits and monitor AI consumption patterns.

I then used a Claude Usage Strategy prompt that analyzed my profile, learning goals, and daily activities.

Based on my responses, Claude generated a personalized usage strategy that recommended:

* The most suitable Claude model for my work
* Appropriate effort levels for different tasks
* Situations where alternative models should be used
* An optimized workflow for learning, coding, research, and project development

---

## What I Learned

One of the most valuable lessons from today's activity was that not every task requires the same AI model or reasoning effort.

Different tasks benefit from different levels of reasoning and processing.

For example:

* Simple questions may require lower effort.
* Learning and coding tasks may require standard or high effort.
* Complex research and strategic planning may benefit from advanced reasoning modes.

Understanding when to use each option can improve both efficiency and output quality.

---

## Claude Counter Exploration

I explored the Claude Counter browser extension and learned how it helps users:

* Monitor Claude usage
* Track message consumption
* Understand usage limits
* Plan AI sessions more effectively

This provided useful insights into managing AI resources efficiently.

---

## My Biggest Insight

My biggest takeaway is that effective AI usage is not only about asking good questions.

It is also about selecting the right model, choosing the appropriate reasoning effort, and using AI strategically based on the task being performed.

The right combination can significantly improve productivity and learning outcomes.

---

## Conclusion

Today's exercise helped me understand how to work more efficiently with Claude.

By matching tasks with the appropriate model and effort level, users can achieve better results while making more effective use of available AI resources.

This knowledge will help me use AI more strategically for learning, research, software development, and future projects.
