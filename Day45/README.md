Day 45 – Decision Workflow Assistant
Objective

The objective of today's experiment was to understand how Artificial Intelligence can support structured decision-making by analyzing different scenarios, evaluating available options, and providing intelligent recommendations. The application focused on improving decision quality through a guided workflow and AI-powered insights.

What I Did

In today's experiment, I generated the Decision Workflow Assistant application as an HTML project using Claude AI. After opening the application in my browser, I explored the complete decision-making workflow by progressing through each stage of the interactive process.

The application guided me through evaluating different options, comparing possible outcomes, and reviewing AI-generated recommendations before reaching a final decision. I carefully observed how each stage contributed to a structured and logical decision-making process.

After completing the workflow, I reviewed the final results dashboard, which summarized the selected decision, supporting analysis, and recommendations. I captured screenshots of each important stage and documented the complete learning experience in my GitHub repository.

Key Learning Outcomes
Understood how AI can support structured decision-making.
Learned the importance of evaluating multiple options before making decisions.
Explored AI-generated recommendations for improving decision quality.
Improved analytical thinking through scenario-based evaluation.
Learned how workflow-based applications simplify complex decision processes.
Strengthened my understanding of AI-powered productivity tools.
