# Day 27 – Prior Authorization Story Simulator

## Objective

The objective of today's experiment was to understand the Prior Authorization process through an interactive story-based simulation. Instead of learning the workflow through a diagram, this activity explained each stage using a real-life patient journey, making the process easier to understand.

---

## What I Did

In this experiment, I generated the Prior Authorization Story Simulator as an HTML application and tested it in my browser. I started Rahul's healthcare journey and followed the complete story from the beginning to the final approval stage.

At every chapter, I read Priya's explanations, selected one of the available options, and observed how each decision influenced the workflow. I completed all eight chapters, monitored the progress bar throughout the story, reviewed the final takeaways, and restarted the simulator to explore a different decision path. Finally, I captured screenshots of the important stages and documented the overall learning.

---

## Key Learning Outcomes

* Learned the Prior Authorization process through an interactive story.
* Understood the responsibilities of patients, healthcare providers, and insurance companies.
* Explored how different decisions affect the authorization workflow.
* Learned the importance of proper documentation and communication during healthcare approvals.
* Understood how storytelling can simplify complex business and healthcare processes.
* Improved my understanding of interactive web applications and user-driven simulations.
