# Day 23 – Customer & MVP Blueprint

## Objective

To understand customer discovery, MVP planning, feature prioritization, pricing strategy, and startup execution using AI-powered business analysis.

## What We Did

Using Claude AI, I continued the startup validation process from Day 22 and generated a complete Customer & MVP Blueprint for the AI Career Mentor for Students startup idea.

The report analyzed:

- Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
- Buyer Persona
- Customer Pain Points
- Customer Journey Mapping
- Buying Triggers & Objections
- MVP Recommendation
- MoSCoW Prioritization
- Pricing Hypothesis
- Risk Analysis
- Founder Action Sheet

## What I Learned

- How to identify the right target customer.
- How to define an Ideal Customer Profile (ICP).
- How customer pain points influence product development.
- The importance of Customer Journey Mapping.
- How MVP features should be prioritized using the MoSCoW framework.
- How startup pricing hypotheses are created and tested.
- How founders can validate ideas before investing resources.

## Key Insights

One of the most valuable lessons from today's experiment was understanding that successful products are built around customer problems, not assumptions.

Before building a product, it is important to understand:
- Who the customer is.
- What problems they face.
- What motivates them to buy.
- Which features create the most value.

Successfully generated a Customer & MVP Blueprint report and explored customer research, MVP planning, pricing strategy, and startup validation frameworks.
