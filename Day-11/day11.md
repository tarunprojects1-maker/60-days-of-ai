# Day 11 – ATS Resume Optimization

## Introduction

Today's activity focused on understanding how Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) evaluate resumes and how resume optimization can improve alignment with specific job roles.

The objective was to compare my existing resume with a target Job Description and identify opportunities to improve ATS compatibility and recruiter readability.

---

## Activity Performed

As part of today's challenge, I uploaded my resume and provided a target Job Description for an AWS Cloud Engineer Intern role.

Using Claude AI, I performed an ATS analysis to evaluate how well my resume matched the requirements of the job description.

The analysis included:

* ATS Match Score
* Gap Analysis
* Missing Keywords
* Missing Skills
* Improvement Opportunities

Based on the analysis, Claude generated an optimized version of my resume while maintaining factual accuracy and preserving all existing experience, projects, skills, and achievements.

---

## ATS Analysis

The ATS evaluation highlighted areas where my resume aligned with the job requirements and identified keywords and skills that were either missing or underrepresented.

This demonstrated how ATS systems scan resumes for relevant keywords and role-specific terminology before a recruiter reviews the application.

---

## What I Learned

Through this activity, I learned:

* How ATS systems evaluate resumes.
* The importance of job-specific keywords.
* How resume structure affects ATS parsing.
* The role of professional summaries in improving relevance.
* How resume optimization can increase visibility during recruitment.

---

## Key Observation

One of the most interesting observations was that small changes in wording, organization, and keyword placement can significantly improve ATS compatibility without changing the factual content of a resume.

---

## Biggest Insight

My biggest takeaway is that a strong resume is not only about skills and projects but also about presenting information in a format that both ATS software and recruiters can easily understand.

Optimizing a resume for a specific role can significantly improve the chances of progressing through the hiring process.

---

## Conclusion

This exercise provided practical experience in ATS-based resume optimization and demonstrated how AI can assist job seekers in improving resume quality, keyword alignment, and overall recruiter readability.

The activity helped me better understand the relationship between job descriptions, ATS systems, and resume effectiveness.
