# Day 25 – AI Shark Tank Simulator

## Overview

Today, I completed the **AI Shark Tank Simulator** project as part of the **60 Days of AI Challenge**. The objective of this task was to build and test an interactive startup pitching application that simulates a real Shark Tank investment meeting using Artificial Intelligence.

Unlike a normal startup presentation, this application asks investor-style questions, evaluates the business idea, analyzes the startup, and generates investment recommendations based on the founder's responses.

---

## Objective

The main goal of this experiment was to understand how AI can simulate real-world startup investment discussions and provide meaningful feedback for entrepreneurs.

---

## What I Did

* Generated the complete AI Shark Tank Simulator HTML application.
* Saved and tested the HTML file in the browser.
* Entered my startup idea: **AI Career Mentor**.
* Filled all startup information including:

  * Startup Name
  * Funding Ask
  * Problem Statement
  * Proposed Solution
  * Revenue Model
  * Target Audience
* Participated in the AI Shark Tank interview.
* Answered investor questions from different AI judges.
* Reviewed the startup scorecard.
* Analyzed the investment recommendation.
* Reviewed valuation and funding suggestions.
* Generated the startup report.
* Captured screenshots of the complete simulation.

---

## What I Learned

This experiment helped me understand that building a startup is much more than having a good idea. Investors evaluate multiple factors before making an investment decision, including market size, customer validation, competition, pricing strategy, revenue model, customer acquisition, scalability, and financial planning.

The AI Shark Tank simulation also improved my ability to answer startup-related questions confidently and think like a founder. It demonstrated how AI can be used to practice business pitching and prepare for real investor meetings.
