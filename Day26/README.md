# Day 26 – Prior Authorization Workflow Simulator

## Objective

The objective of this experiment was to understand how the Prior Authorization (PA) process works in the healthcare industry by interacting with a workflow simulator. The activity focused on learning how a patient's treatment request moves through different stages before receiving approval from the insurance provider.

---

## What I Did

In this experiment, I generated the Prior Authorization Workflow Simulator as an HTML application and opened it in my browser. I started a new patient case involving an elective knee replacement procedure and followed each stage of the authorization workflow.

I verified the patient's insurance details, completed the medical necessity review, gathered all required clinical documents, submitted the authorization request, and tracked the request through the payer review process. During the simulation, I observed how the workflow changes based on different review stages and how documentation affects the approval process.

I also explored the workflow tracker, monitored the progress across different days, reviewed the educational explanations provided by the simulator, and tested the complete workflow before recording the results with screenshots.

---

## Key Learning Outcomes

* Understood the complete Prior Authorization workflow used in healthcare.
* Learned the importance of medical necessity documentation before treatment approval.
* Explored how insurance providers review and process authorization requests.
* Understood the role of supporting documents in reducing approval delays.
* Learned how provider, patient, and payer interact throughout the authorization lifecycle.
* Improved my understanding of workflow-based web applications and interactive simulations.
